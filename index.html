WHY THIS EXISTS

Learning shouldn't stop when the school day ends.

Parents want to help. Kids need explanations that make sense. And sometimes the right practice tool is hard to find.

That's why this project exists: to create approachable, free learning experiences that give children more chances to practice and families more ways to support them—without putting another cost in the way.

I'm Christopher Bazemore—a dad, educator, technology professional, and U.S. Navy veteran who believes technology should help people learn, not get in their way.

As a parent helping my own children navigate schoolwork, I've seen how much a clear explanation, extra practice, and a little encouragement can matter. I started creating practical learning tools to address those everyday challenges.

My background in education and IT shapes how I build: simple enough for kids to use, helpful enough for parents to trust, and flexible enough to keep improving.

This site is my way of sharing those tools with the community—for free. If something I've built helps one more student understand a skill or one more parent feel equipped to help, it's worth doing.
